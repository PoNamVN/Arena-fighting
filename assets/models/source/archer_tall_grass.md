Tall forest grass authored in Blender 5.2 through Blender MCP.

Seven curved, folded ribbon leaves per tuft, 140 triangles, 1.20 m maximum
height before runtime variation. Base origin, meter scale, Y-up glTF export.
Vertex colors blend dark forest roots into olive green tips. Runtime scale
varies between 0.9 and 1.35 for grass approximately 1.1–1.6 m tall.

archer_tall_grass.glb is the editable interchange asset. The matching triangle
data in archer_tall_grass.mesh.json is loaded by ArcherForestEscape into a
shared ArrayMesh, so standalone runtime tests do not require an editor import.
The Godot shader adds breeze, two-sided lighting and directional parting.
